# Open problems

A loop that stops changing is only the first requirement. We also need the same equations and answer under different schedules, types that admit runtime values, and affordable re-checking after edits. The [facts](facts.md) and [attempts](attempts.md) distinguish pinned measurements, executable models and proofs. Older measurements must be refreshed before describing current main.

1. **Discover the same equations.** Dispatch targets, return and parameter slots, closure slots and their writers are discovered during inference. Different schedules can therefore solve different systems (F5, F16). Can the complete logical structure be declared from the program, or discovered monotonically without retracting edges or changing reference routing? The check is matching final structure digests across shuffled schedules, complete read/write membership, matching carried-state answers and zero extra-round movement. Predeclaration additionally requires matching start/end structure. The October 10 gate still fails on all five apps ([F24](facts.md#f24)); the broader logical dump finds writer/route differences on all five and slot differences on four, with repeat-varying routes and mapping gaps limiting attribution ([F31](facts.md#f31)). The October 11 fixture declaration envelope is killed by 18 missing memberships despite stable declaration/attempt sets; the covered Campfire split supports a narrower mutation-history explanation ([F33](facts.md#f33)). Complete raw membership remains open. Its continuation covers all eighteen saved fixture memberships but leaves raw exhaustion and justified routing incomplete ([F40](facts.md#f40)). A reflective write in an evaluated Array child also escapes the native harvest walker ([F43](facts.md#f43)); complete equation coverage remains open. October 15 narrows the spliced ownership race on Campfire ([F44](facts.md#f44)), while invoked closures still omit executed writes ([F47](facts.md#f47)). The ownership composition exposes a storage-law failure and its bounded repair; ownership alone changes larger-app contributions ([F51](facts.md#f51), [F52](facts.md#f52), [F53](facts.md#f53)). Closure application is now bounded by completed fixture/app gates, while the declared join still changes aliases and module values ([F57](facts.md#f57), [F58](facts.md#f58)).

2. **Make every writer obey its algebra.** The merged parameter and ivar join repairs are useful foundations, but return replacement, narrowing and history-sensitive size cuts remain separate obligations. For each carried slot, declare its join and its writer transfers. At the join, contributor permutations and regroupings must give the same result: the join must be commutative, associative and idempotent, with pending as its identity. Source-priority transfers, which prefer one writer over another, are not joins and need their own justification: their priority semantics, monotonicity as inputs grow, and preservation of possible values. Widening must preserve possible values too. F14 shows why better precision counts alone cannot validate a merge rule. The finite writer-law instrument records remaining counterexamples after the merged repairs, without certifying the whole carried state ([F25](facts.md#f25)).
