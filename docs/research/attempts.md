## Long-name dispatch

The historical scanner excluded string literals longer than 80 bytes, although literal `send`
dispatch accepted them. A 94-character ASCII method name in a three-file chain escaped dependency
tracking: skipping left its caller unresolved, an extra round changed state, and shuffled visits
changed the answer. Removing the cutoff repaired the fixture; staged prototype [`92844f68`](https://github.com/bunnykong/roundhouse/tree/92844f68ea0bef9bfb6d8a8f1b0fc4cad4fff51b) includes
that fix. Retain the fixture and compare actual dispatch lookup names with scanned names. The [five public apps at the lab pins](https://github.com/bunnykong/roundhouse-fixpoint-lab/blob/d824c4355c7faae4b9bb4a77b25daa178a3df36c/corpus/apps.json) contained no such long literal calls, so this particular defect did not affect
their recorded results.

[retained fixture](https://github.com/bunnykong/roundhouse-fixpoint-lab/blob/d824c4355c7faae4b9bb4a77b25daa178a3df36c/reproductions/inference-fixtures.md#long-name-dispatch); repair included at [`92844f68`](https://github.com/bunnykong/roundhouse/tree/92844f68ea0bef9bfb6d8a8f1b0fc4cad4fff51b).

## Writer-rule implementation boundaries

In the historical shape prototype `e00feac2`, setter dispatch retained a separate diagnostic verdict
after refusal annotations, and concatenation used the shared collection view for tuple operands.
The accompanying structure prototype `f7694daa` introduced closure result references whose consumers
had to inspect the referenced head before checking pending state. Its narrowing identities also
needed to avoid synthetic locals shadowing unbound method calls. Local regression tests cover these
integration cases. They do not establish the complete structure contract or order independence.

## Budget-dependent expansion cache

The focused witness for the budget-cache lead makes a later small root inherit `Array[untyped]`
from a slot expanded under an earlier large root's exhausted budget; a fresh expander gives
`Array[Array[String]]`. The [`520ef142`](https://github.com/bunnykong/roundhouse/tree/520ef142b4a4d21235f63b6c5c4a23e5155962fa) candidate with `RH_FOLD_NOCACHE_BUDGET=1` avoids memoizing
budget-dependent results. Its focused regression passes and checks retained budget, gradual,
guarded-cycle, and missing-slot uncertainty. On the five public apps at the lab pins, no baseline S3 category loss was
proved to originate from this defect: the first 31 suspected cases already contained `untyped`
before expansion. Candidate corpus effects remain unmeasured.

**Source:** [Expansion regression and pre-expansion corpus cases](https://github.com/bunnykong/roundhouse-fixpoint-lab/blob/d824c4355c7faae4b9bb4a77b25daa178a3df36c/receipts/expansion-cache/README.md).
